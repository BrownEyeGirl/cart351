Wired Article About Xanadu Stands (1995)

Ted Nelson's Xanadu Stands were supposed to revolutionize open source global libraries of hypertext, placing mini information kiosks like payphones in public areas. Inspired by the way McDonalds franchised stores, Nelson believe that these would change the world. This truly emulates the open source, shared, optimistic ethos of early internet that I am so drawn to and inspired by.
