The Tetrad of Media Effects, McLuhan's 4-part plan for media integration

Enhancement: what does the medium enhance or amplify? eg. internet devices enhance instantanious communication and personal accessibility
Obsolescence: what older media is made obsolete by the new medium? eg. rip landlines, physical maps, watches, cameras
Retrieval: what earlier medium or cultural ground is being revived? eg. emojis bring back tribal visual culture, dms/text based communication is amplified over phonecalls
Reversal: when pushed to extremes and widely adopted, what original characteristics of the medium are reverse? eg. communication devices bring isolation and loneliness, digital burnout
